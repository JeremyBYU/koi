Pond theme preview

Every theme in themes/, rendered with the same pond layout and the same three koi (sanke, asagi, and an ogon over the shallows, the hardest place to see a gold fish). This is a mock of the shader, not the game: close enough to compare palettes and looks. Rebuild after editing a theme with python3 research/style/build_preview.py. Click a pond to open it large and animated. Chips copy their hex on click.

In the large view: t/T next/previous scene, l time of day in the family, p pixel size, space pause, Esc close.
