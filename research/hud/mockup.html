Pond HUD mockup

A calm, hidden-by-default HUD for the koi pond. Click the pond to give it the keyboard, then try Tab, 1 to 5, n, t, l and ?. Hover reveal is off by default, as in the spec; turn it on below to try the 0.5 s dwell. While the pond has focus it keeps Tab; click outside it to get the page back. Spec: research/hud/SPEC.md.

What to notice

  • Nothing is on screen by default. A key shows only the pebble it changed, with a one-word label, for 3 s.
  • The row fades in over 0.25 s and out over 0.6 s in 4 visible steps, the way the terminal version would re-send the image 4 times.
  • Text on the HUD is drawn in the terminal font on whole cells and appears only on the last fade steps, because real terminal text cannot fade.
  • The pixel scenes draw the pond at the theme's own pixel_px (3 for Rice Paper Noon, 6 for Lantern Dusk, 5 for Pocket Moss) and the HUD at 2 px, so the small icons stay readable. No soft edges anywhere in the pixel HUD. The text stays crisp.
  • The expanded row has no key hints. Hover a pebble (with hover reveal on) to see its key; ? lists them all.
  • Time of day uses the theme system's seven times. The time tray and l only offer the times the current scene has: Garden has four, Pixel Garden two, Pocket Moss one.
  • The readout counts HUD image sends. It only goes up when something on the HUD changes; an open, idle HUD sends nothing.
  • Each food behaves differently. Flakes are shared slowly, petals get a curious look, seeds pull koi down, and the treat is circled by the whole pond. Seeds and the treat are phase two in the spec.
